The Economics Discipline Group at Deakin University will host the 22nd Australasian Development Economics Workshop (ADEW 2027) in Melbourne on Thursday 10 and Friday 11 June 2027.
ADEW is an annual workshop that brings together development economists from around Australia and internationally.
Dilip Mookherjee of Boston University will give the keynote.

The call for papers is open to researchers at all career stages, including early career researchers and PhD students.
Submit a full paper and an abstract of up to 300 words by 30 March 2027; authors will hear back by 15 April 2027.
Early bird registration closes on 30 April 2027.

Details and the submission link are on the [ADEW 2027 website](https://sites.google.com/view/adew2027).
