The Sydney Development Economics Group (SDEG) held its latest workshop at the UNSW Business School on Thursday 22 October 2026.
The afternoon combined presentations of recent research in development economics with a discussion session on the economics job market, grants, and work-life balance.

SDEG connects Sydney-based researchers interested in development economics.
Details of future workshops and the group's mailing list are on the [SDEG website](https://www.sydneydevecon.org/).
