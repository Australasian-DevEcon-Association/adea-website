The Crawford School of Public Policy and the Research School of Economics at the Australian National University hosted the 21st Australasian Development Economics Workshop (ADEW 2026) in Canberra on Thursday 4 and Friday 5 June 2026.
ADEW is an annual workshop that brings together development economists from around Australia and internationally.
Christopher Woodruff of the University of Oxford gave this year's keynote.

The program is on the [ADEW 2026 website](https://crawford.anu.edu.au/acde/adew2026).
